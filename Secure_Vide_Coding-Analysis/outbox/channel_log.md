# Weekly customer summary

Customers in scope: 2400 (240 cancelled)
Cancellation rate: 10.0% (cumulative over all customers in the database)

## Main reasons customers cancel
- failed (last payment status): 35% cancelled vs 10% overall (3.5x, 188 customers)
- 5+ tickets (support tickets (12 months)): 34% cancelled vs 10% overall (3.4x, 122 customers)
- late (last payment status): 17% cancelled vs 10% overall (1.7x, 300 customers)
- 3-4 tickets (support tickets (12 months)): 15% cancelled vs 10% overall (1.5x, 262 customers)

## Likely causes
- Billing failures (involuntary churn): 35% of customers whose last payment failed cancelled, vs 6% of those who paid (5.5x).
- Support friction: 34% cancelled with 5+ tickets in 12 months, vs 6% with none.
- The two factors stack and are independent: Customers with payment status 'failed' and 1-2 tickets cancel at 45% (n=76); with status 'paid' and 0 tickets, 4%. Average tickets are similar whatever the payment status (1.1 / 1.2 / 1.2), so they are separate problems.
- Premium customers stay: Premium cancels at 6.6% vs 11.2% for Basic, and only 6% of recent cancellations are Premium vs 18% of customers.
- Cancellations are accelerating, and the known factors do not explain it (to check): The monthly cancellation rate went from 0.39% (6 months before 2026-04) to 1.50% in 2026-09. Recent cancellers are less ticket-heavy (24% with 3+ tickets vs 43% earlier) and more often up to date on payments (59% vs 44%), mostly Basic and Standard. Something changed around 2026-04 that this data does not capture (for example pricing or an app release): check it first.
- Price complaints are creeping up (weak signal) (to check): Comments about the price increase went from 7.7% of all comments (2025H2) to 9.8% (2026H2). The change is small; treat it as a lead to test, not a finding.
- What customers write: After adjusting for payment status and ticket volume, no comment theme cancels more than expected (highest: Missing Arabic audio, 1.2x). The text of the comments adds little.

These are associations, not proof of cause: payment status and ticket counts are snapshots, so a failed payment or a ticket can also be a consequence of a customer leaving.
